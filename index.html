About

Alhassan Abdulai is a climate finance and carbon markets professional focused on integrity-first systems for measurement, reporting, and market participation. He connects technical MRV design, policy incentives, and governance requirements so projects and decision-makers can move from data to trusted action.

He holds an Erasmus Mundus MSc in Environmental Sciences, Policy and Management (Distinction, GPA 3.73) across Central European University, the University of the Aegean, and the University of Manchester, complemented by an MSc in Climate Change Engineering from PAUWES and a First Class BSc in Agribusiness from Ghana. That combination grounds his work in market design, geospatial analysis, and field delivery.

Professionally, he has supported UNFCCC transparency work, SEforALL’s Africa Carbon Markets Initiative, and climate-finance programme delivery with the Common Fund for Commodities. He builds decision-ready outputs—briefs, stakeholder materials, and reproducible analysis—that translate evidence into implementation without sacrificing auditability.
