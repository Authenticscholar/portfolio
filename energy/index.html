About

Alhassan Abdulai works on energy access, energy markets, and energy transition delivery. He builds decision-ready analysis and partner materials for clean-energy pathways—from distributed renewable energy and diesel genset displacement to industrial CCS economics under emissions trading.

At Sustainable Energy for All (SEforALL), he supported the Africa Carbon Markets Initiative as High Integrity Project Development Officer: methodology research for fossil-fuel displacement, Mission 300–aligned electrification analysis, Carbon Hub / digital platform delivery for African energy developers, and market-infrastructure research on verification readiness.

His Erasmus Mundus MSc (Distinction, GPA 3.73) spans Central European University, the University of the Aegean, and the University of Manchester, with coursework in Energy Markets and Energy Transition and a thesis on Peak Cluster CCS viability against UK ETS pricing. He complements that with an MSc in Climate Change Engineering (PAUWES) and a First Class BSc in Agribusiness.
